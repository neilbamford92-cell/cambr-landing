Beta programme

Running the beta.

Cambr scores Britain's roads for how good they are to drive, and plans routes along the best of them. The beta is how we find out where the scores and routes are right, wrong or missing, from people who drive them. This page changes with every update, so it's worth bookmarking.

1 How to install

iPhone (iOS 15.1 or later): Cambr runs through Apple's TestFlight app. When your invite arrives, install TestFlight from the App Store, open Apple's email on your iPhone, tap View in TestFlight, then Install. Updates arrive through TestFlight.

Android: Cambr runs through Google Play's testing programme. When your invite arrives, open it on your Android phone, signed in to the Google account you gave us. Tap Join the Cambr beta, accept the invite, then install Cambr from Google Play. If Play says "Item not found", wait up to an hour and try again. Updates arrive through the Play Store.

Then sign in (Profile, then Sign in) with Apple, Google or your email, so your drives and ratings follow you.

Not in yet?

The beta is now full, and the last group of testers is joining this week. If you didn't get a place, Cambr launches on the App Store and Google Play by the end of November, and early-access sign-ups hear first.

2 What's in the beta now

  • Map: scored roads across Great Britain, graded S, A and B, with each road's score and what drives it. The best roads are named at wide zoom, alongside car spots (circuits, museums, cafés and more, searchable from the map) and pink dots for 147 great European roads coming soon.
  • Routing: four options for every trip (Fastest, Quick, Driver's and Extended) and up to 5 stops, with voice and lane guidance, re-routing, live arrival times and a heads-up before heavy traffic. Roundabouts name the exit you need.
  • Convoys (new): drive a route with friends. Drive, then Multiplayer: invite with a link or QR code, plan the route together, then see everyone's car on the map as you go. Your position is shared only during the drive, and never near your home.
  • Build your own: pick the roads you want and Cambr joins them up, one way or back to where you started, or generate a loop from where you are. Pick a road as your destination and Cambr takes you to its far end.
  • Discover: Drives and Roads, with 46 hand-picked drives and loops, multi-day road trips, famous trips and the best roads near you.
  • On the road: speed limits, camera warnings (one alert per camera) and the score of the road you're on. Your drive keeps recording, and directions keep talking, with the phone locked. Turn your phone sideways on any screen.
  • Drives and routes: drive logs you can save, share or keep private, with a privacy filter around home. Name and edit your saved routes, and export them to Google Maps or as a GPX.
  • Roads: save, tag, mark as not worth it, and rate out of 10. Report rough surfaces and potholes, and nominate roads we haven't scored.
  • Feed: likes, comments and notifications, driver profiles with a photo and bio, and photos on your drives and cars (number plates can be blurred for you). Tag a road in a caption, edit a post after posting, and share a road as a picture card (iPhone).
  • Account: sign in with Apple, Google or email, pick a username, and add your cars to the garage. Report or block from the ⋯ menu in the Feed.

4 Known issues (updated 12 November)

  • Long routes can take up to a minute to calculate.
  • Some areas still have few scored roads, so routes there are thinner. The app tells you when a trip has few great roads and offers a better one.
  • In heavy traffic the arrival time can jump around, and traffic warnings can arrive once you're already in the queue. Fixes are on the way.
  • On Android, the car and map can point the wrong way while you're stopped. A fix is on the way.
  • On Android, the drive notification sometimes shows only “Cambr”. Your drive still records.
  • On Android, if Google sign-in doesn't work, use email sign-in and tell us.
  • Speed limits can be blank or wrong on some minor roads, and a few recently lowered limits still show the old one. Tell us when you spot one.
  • Routes stay in Great Britain for now.

5 Sending feedback

Three ways in, and all of them take seconds. The app attaches your build, phone, the road, where you were and the drive log to every report, so you never need to share diagnostics or type an email address.

While driving

Two buttons on the right of the screen. The heart tags the road you are on; the thumbs-down marks it as a bad road. One tap each, no typing.

After a drive

The summary asks you to rate the roads you drove, out of 10, starting with the ones you thumbed. A number and a tap on what stood out is enough, and it is what builds the map for everyone. Underneath it, "Something wrong on this drive?" opens a report with that drive attached.

Any time

Open Profile and tap Send feedback. A problem, a road that is wrong or missing, an idea, anything. On any road's page, Report this road does the same with that road already filled in.

Screenshots and email

On iPhone, take a screenshot, tap it, choose Share Beta Feedback and it reaches us through Apple. Email works too: hello@cambr.uk. You will hear back, and every update lists what was fixed.

Most useful of all: the roads you know. A road that is missing, scored wrong, or really a farm track. Give us the road name and the area; a road number alone is often ambiguous.

6 The deal

The beta is free. Founding-member pricing, for life, goes to the first 100 people who create a Cambr account and use the beta: drive with it, rate roads and send feedback. We'll confirm who qualifies before launch. The most active testers also get first access to new features.
