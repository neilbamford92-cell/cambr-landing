Beta programme

Running the beta.

Everything you need in one place. This page changes as the build does — bookmark it rather than the email.

1 How to install

TestFlight steps go here. Worth covering: install TestFlight from the App Store first, then the invite link and/or redeem code, what to do if the link says the beta is full, and roughly how big the download is. A numbered <ol> list is the right shape for this one.

2 What's in this build

The features that are live and worth trying. Keep it to what a driver can actually go and do this weekend — the map, scores and tiers, the routing modes, the loop generator. A <ul> works well here.

3 What's not in this build yet

Set expectations before they find out the hard way. Anything the landing page marks "Soon" belongs here, plus anything half-built. Saying this plainly up front is what stops it arriving as a bug report.

4 Known issues

The list you'll rewrite most often. Known bugs, so nobody spends their evening reporting something already on the board. Worth noting the date each one was added, and striking items once they're fixed.

5 How to send feedback

Where it goes and what makes a report useful. If there's an in-app route, lead with it; otherwise an email address. Asking for the road name plus region saves real time — road numbers alone are ambiguous.

6 The deal

Free through the beta, Founding Member status at launch. Spell out what Founding Member actually gets them and how long it lasts, and what you're asking in return.
