Beta programme

Running the beta.

Everything you need in one place. This page changes as the build does — bookmark it rather than the email.

1 How to install

  1. On your iPhone (iOS 15.1 or later), install TestFlight from the App Store. It is Apple's free app for betas.
  2. Open the invite link from your email, or the public link you were sent, on the phone. Tap Accept, then Install. The download is about 80 MB.
  3. Open Cambr from your home screen like any other app. TestFlight keeps it up to date: when a new build lands you get a notification, and by default it installs itself.
  4. If the link says the beta is full, email hello@cambr.uk and we will make room.

Android: not yet. iOS came first because that is where Cambr was built and tested; Android is in the plan and the people who told us they are on Android are the first to hear.

2 What's in this build

  • The map. Thousands of scored roads across 37 regions of Great Britain, in three tiers: S (the best in the country), A (worth a detour) and B (a good road). Tap any road for its score, what makes it up, and what other drivers say.
  • Routing. Search a destination and choose Fastest or Driver's Route, which threads the best scored roads it can fit into your time. Turn-by-turn voice guidance, re-routing when you leave the line, and arrival times from live road data.
  • On the road. The speed-limit sign for the road you are on, warnings for fixed cameras, average-speed zones and (in Scotland) mobile camera sites, and the name and score of the road under you.
  • Your drives. Every drive is logged with its track and the scored roads you covered. Save a drive to your profile, share it to the feed, or keep it to yourself. Set a home location and the app hides the stretch near home on anything you share.
  • Your roads. Save roads to find again, tag a road you love while driving, mark a road as bad, and rate roads out of 10 after a drive.
  • Your account. Sign in with your email so your saved roads and drives survive a new phone. No password; a link in your inbox.

3 What's not in this build yet

  • Android. See above.
  • Loop routes ("the best 90 minutes from here, back to here") and Scenic Route as a separate mode. Both are designed, neither is built.
  • Convoy and the walkie-talkie. Greyed out in the app for now.
  • Offline maps and CarPlay. Not in the beta.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google. Email only for now.
  • Coverage. Some areas have few scored roads yet, and Ireland is thin. If your local favourite is missing, that is exactly the feedback we want (see 5).

4 Known issues

  • 1 Oct — Working out a route can take up to about 50 seconds for long trips or routes through cities. It is being worked on; the answer is worth the wait.
  • 1 Oct — The speed-limit sign can be blank on some minor roads and show the wrong limit on a few. Tell us the road.
  • 1 Oct — Average-speed camera zones are thinner in some areas than others. Report any you drive through that the app did not call.
  • 1 Oct — The car marker is large in light mode. Cosmetic.
  • 1 Oct — On a weak signal a re-route can take a few seconds to arrive. Improved in the latest build; still tell us if it stalls.

5 How to send feedback

Two ways, and both take seconds.

  1. While driving: the heart tags the road you are on; the thumbs-down marks it as a bad road. One tap each, no typing.
  2. Anything else: tap Send feedback. It is at the end of every drive ("Something wrong on this drive?"), on every road's page ("Report this road"), and in your Profile. Pick what it is, type a line, send. The app attaches your build, phone, the road, where you were and the drive log, so you never need to share diagnostics or type an email address.

After a drive, the app asks you to rate the roads you drove, out of 10, starting with the ones you thumbed. A number and a tap on what stood out is enough, and it is what builds the map for everyone.

Screenshots: take one, tap it, choose Share Beta Feedback and it reaches us through Apple. Email works too: hello@cambr.uk. You will hear back, and every update lists what was fixed.

Most useful of all: the roads you know. A road that is missing, scored wrong, or really a farm track. Give us the road name and the area; a road number alone is often ambiguous.

6 The deal

Cambr is free for the whole beta. Everyone testing it is counted among the first hundred sign-ups, which means founding-member pricing, locked for life, when the paid tier arrives.

In return we ask three things: drive with it, thumb the roads, and tell us when something is wrong. The scores are built from data; what makes them right is people who know the roads.
