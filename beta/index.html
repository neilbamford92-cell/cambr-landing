Beta programme

Running the beta.

Everything you need in one place. This page changes as the build does — bookmark it rather than the email.

Thanks for joining. Cambr scores roads by how good they are to drive and routes you along the best of them. The scores come from data: road geometry, elevation, speed limits, cameras, what the motoring press has written. What the data cannot tell us is whether a road is actually good on the ground. That is what this beta is for: real drivers on real roads, telling us where the map is right, where it is wrong, and what is missing.

You are in the first group through the door. Over the next few weeks you will get new builds through TestFlight as fixes and features land, and this page is updated with each one. Everything you do in the app, a heart on a road, a rating, a report, comes straight to us and shapes what ships next.

1 How to install

  1. On your iPhone (iOS 15.1 or later), install TestFlight from the App Store. It is Apple's free app for betas.
  2. Open the invite link from your email, or the public link you were sent, on the phone. Tap Accept, then Install. The download is about 80 MB.
  3. Open Cambr from your home screen like any other app. TestFlight keeps it up to date: when a new build lands you get a notification, and by default it installs itself.
  4. If the link says the beta is full, email hello@cambr.uk and we will make room.

Not on iPhone?

Android is not in this beta. iOS came first because that is where Cambr was built and tested. Android is in the plan, and the people who told us they are on Android will be the first to hear.

2 What's in this build

  • The map. Thousands of scored roads across 37 regions of Great Britain, in three tiers: S (the best in the country), A (worth a detour) and B (a good road). Tap any road for its score, what makes it up, and what other drivers say.
  • Routing. Search a destination and choose Fastest or Driver's Route, which threads the best scored roads it can fit into your time. Turn-by-turn voice guidance, re-routing when you leave the line, and arrival times from live road data.
  • On the road. The speed-limit sign for the road you are on, warnings for fixed cameras, average-speed zones and (in Scotland) mobile camera sites, and the name and score of the road under you.
  • Your drives. Every drive is logged with its track and the scored roads you covered. Save a drive to your profile, share it to the feed, or keep it to yourself. Set a home location and the app hides the stretch near home on anything you share.
  • Your roads. Save roads to find again, tag a road you love while driving, mark a road as bad, and rate roads out of 10 after a drive.
  • Your account. Sign in with your email so your saved roads and drives survive a new phone. No password; a link in your inbox.

3 What's not in this build yet

  • Android. See above.
  • Routes with stops. Today a route has a start and a destination only. Multiple waypoints are one of the first things we will add.
  • AI Route Planner (describe the drive you want in words) and the Tunnel Finder. Both designed, neither in this build.
  • Loop routes ("the best 90 minutes from here, back to here") and Scenic Route as a separate mode.
  • Convoy and the walkie-talkie. Greyed out in the app for now.
  • Offline maps and CarPlay. Not in the beta.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google. Email only for now.
  • Coverage. Some areas have few scored roads yet, and Ireland is thin. If your local favourite is missing, that is exactly the feedback we want (see 5).

4 Known issues

Added 1 October

  • Working out a route can take up to about 50 seconds for long trips or routes through cities. It is being worked on; the answer is worth the wait.
  • The speed-limit sign can be blank on some minor roads and show the wrong limit on a few. Tell us the road.
  • Average-speed camera zones are thinner in some areas than others. Report any you drive through that the app did not call.
  • The car marker is large in light mode. Cosmetic.
  • On a weak signal a re-route can take a few seconds to arrive. Improved in the latest build; still tell us if it stalls.

5 How to send feedback

Three ways in, and all of them take seconds. The app attaches your build, phone, the road, where you were and the drive log to every report, so you never need to share diagnostics or type an email address.

While driving

Two buttons on the right of the screen. The heart tags the road you are on; the thumbs-down marks it as a bad road. One tap each, no typing.

After a drive

The summary asks you to rate the roads you drove, out of 10, starting with the ones you thumbed. A number and a tap on what stood out is enough, and it is what builds the map for everyone. Underneath it, "Something wrong on this drive?" opens a report with that drive attached.

Any time

Open Profile and tap Send feedback. A problem, a road that is wrong or missing, an idea, anything. On any road's page, Report this road does the same with that road already filled in.

Screenshots and email

Take a screenshot, tap it, choose Share Beta Feedback and it reaches us through Apple. Email works too: hello@cambr.uk. You will hear back, and every update lists what was fixed.

Most useful of all: the roads you know. A road that is missing, scored wrong, or really a farm track. Give us the road name and the area; a road number alone is often ambiguous.

6 The deal

Cambr is free for the whole beta.

Founding-member pricing, locked for life, goes to the testers who actually test: drive with it, rate the roads, send feedback. Install it and never open it again, and the offer does not apply. We are not counting hours. We are looking for people who used it on real roads and told us what they found.

The testers who use it most and send the most feedback are also first in line for the new features as they land: everything in section 3, from routes with stops to the AI Route Planner, goes to them before anyone else.
