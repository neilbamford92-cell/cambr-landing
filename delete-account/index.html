Delete your Cambr account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and everything linked to it straight away: drives, saved roads, ratings, road tags, garage and username.

Don’t have the app any more? Email hello@cambr.uk from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

Backups are kept for up to 30 days, then removed.

If you have a Cambr Pro subscription, cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first. Deleting your account does not stop billing.
