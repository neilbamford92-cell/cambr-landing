Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · Applies to the Cambr iOS app (beta) and cambr.uk

Cambr is a driving app that scores roads for driving quality and guides you along the best ones. To do that it needs your location, and it keeps a record of the drives and roads you choose to save. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

1. Who we are

The data controller is Cambr Technologies Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17371232), registered office 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ. Contact: hello@cambr.uk.

4. What other users can see

Nothing, unless you choose it. A drive is private by default. If you set a drive’s visibility to “Everyone”, its title, distance, duration, roads, route overview and any note or photos appear in the public feed. Road reviews you post are shown with the road. You can delete a drive or review at any time.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Deleting your account (Profile → Settings → Delete account) removes your account and every record linked to it — drives, saved roads, routes, garage, reviews, captures and feedback — immediately and permanently. Anonymous accounts that have never saved anything may be removed after 90 days of inactivity. Backups are retained for up to 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to or restrict how we use it. Deletion is built into the app; for anything else email hello@cambr.uk and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Location permission

You can change or withdraw location access at any time in iOS Settings → Cambr → Location. Without it the app still shows the map and road scores, but cannot guide you or record drives. Background location is used only while a drive with guidance is active, so instructions keep coming if you switch apps or lock the phone.

8. Children

Cambr is for drivers and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Every record is tied to your account and protected by row-level access rules, so no other user can read or change your data. The app never contains server credentials.

10. Changes

We will update this page when the app’s data use changes and note the date at the top. Material changes during the beta will also be announced in the app.
