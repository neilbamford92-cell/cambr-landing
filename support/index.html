Contact us

  • Email hello@cambr.uk.
  • In the app: Profile → Settings → Send feedback.
  • Something went wrong on a drive, such as the directions, a re-route or an alert: Profile → Settings → Share drive diagnostics. It opens your phone’s share sheet with the drive log, so you can send it to hello@cambr.uk. The log stays on your phone until you share it.

Cambr Pro

Cambr Pro is an optional subscription, bought and managed through the App Store or Google Play. New subscribers get a 14-day free trial.

  • Start: tap a Pro feature in the app, such as Start drive on a planned route, and choose a plan.
  • Cancel or change your plan: on iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Cambr. On Android, open Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Cambr. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends, or it renews automatically.
  • New phone, or Pro not showing? Open a Pro feature and tap Restore purchases on the Cambr Pro screen, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you paid with.
  • Deleting your Cambr account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by Cambr. Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google through the Google Play Help Center.

Where Cambr works

Cambr works in the UK and Ireland.

Location

Cambr asks for your location to show the driving roads near you, guide you along your route and, if you join a convoy, share your position with the other drivers in it.

You can say no, or turn location off later in your phone’s settings. You can still browse the map, road scores, reviews and the Feed, but Cambr can’t show where you are, record a drive or guide you along a route.

Drive safely

Obey speed limits, road signs and the Highway Code. Set your route before you set off and keep your phone in a fixed holder. Speed camera alerts are a guide, not a guarantee: a missing alert does not mean there is no camera.
