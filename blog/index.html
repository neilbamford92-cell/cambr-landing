Notes from the map.

Long reads on what makes a road worth driving, guides to the ones that are, and the occasional argument with the received wisdom.

Some of it comes out of measuring every road in Britain. The rest comes out of going and driving them. Either way the aim is the same: to point you at the roads worth the detour, including the ones nobody has bothered to write about.
