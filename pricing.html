You never hit a paywall mid-drive. Once a drive has started, re-routes keep working.

How the 14-day trial works.

  1. Day 1. You pick a plan in the app and Pro unlocks straight away. Apple or Google takes your payment details; nothing is charged.
  2. Up to 24 hours before it ends. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play settings and you pay nothing.
  3. Day 14. If you have not cancelled, the store charges the plan you chose (£39.99 a year or £4.99 a month) and it renews until you cancel.

Questions about Pro.

Is Cambr free?

Yes. The map, road scores, reviews, saving roads, the Feed, Free Drive, planning a route, driving the Fastest route and joining a convoy stay free. There is no time limit on the free app.

What does Cambr Pro add?

Turn-by-turn navigation on Cambr's Quick, Driver's and Extended routes, the route builder with stops, driving loops, leading a convoy, and importing and exporting routes.

How much is Cambr Pro?

£39.99 a year or £4.99 a month in the UK, €49.99 a year or €5.99 a month in Ireland, VAT included. Billed by Apple or Google. The app shows the exact price before you buy.

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers get 14 days free. It starts when you choose it, and the store charges you when it ends unless you cancel before then.

14 days free, then £39.99 a year, renews automatically until cancelled. Cancel any time in your store account settings.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, open Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Cambr. On Android, open Google Play, your profile picture, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, Cambr. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends, or it renews automatically. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by Cambr. Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google through the Google Play Help Center. Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected.

Can I buy Pro on this website?

No. Cambr Pro is only sold inside the app, through the App Store or Google Play.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. After that the free app carries on as before, and your saved roads, saved routes and drives stay in your account.

Will a paywall interrupt a drive?

No. Once a drive has started, re-routes keep working.

Do my friends need Pro to join my convoy?

No. Leading a convoy is Pro. Joining is always free.

I use an iPhone and an Android phone. Do I pay twice?

No. Cambr Pro is linked to your Cambr account, so it works on both when you sign in to the same one.

I have a new phone, or Pro is not showing.

Open a Pro feature and tap Restore purchases on the Cambr Pro screen, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you paid with.

Does deleting my Cambr account cancel Pro?

Deleting your Cambr account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first.

Does Pro include CarPlay, Android Auto or offline maps?

Not yet. They are on the way, and they will be part of Cambr Pro. Until offline maps arrive, Cambr needs a mobile signal to navigate.

Where does Cambr work?

Cambr works in the UK and Ireland. 147 of the best driving roads in Europe are on the map too, from Spain, France and Italy to Switzerland, Austria, Germany and Norway. Routing in Europe is coming very soon.
